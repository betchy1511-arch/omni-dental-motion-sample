About Dr. Keihani

Meet Dr. Keihani

After completing his education at the USC School of Dentistry, Dr. Keihani worked as a general dentist in Beverly Hills, and more recently in Oxnard.

To enhance both the health and appearance of his patients’ smiles, he offers a full range of general, restorative and cosmetic dentistry treatments.

He keeps up to date on new technology, new dental procedures and the latest advancements in restorative and cosmetic dentistry, and continues to advance his clinical skills through continuing education courses.
