Nyeland Acres at a glance

Nyeland Acres Sits Outside the City of Oxnard

Nyeland Acres is a community outside the city limits of Oxnard. These facts come from a 2023 Ventura LAFCo review of the City of Oxnard.

Unincorporated
Ventura LAFCo's review of the City of Oxnard names Nyeland Acres as an unincorporated community of Ventura County.
Sphere of influence
The community lies within the City of Oxnard's sphere of influence, to the north of the city.
Fire and police
Fire protection comes from the Ventura County Fire Protection District and the City of Oxnard under a mutual aid agreement. Police protection comes from the Ventura County Sheriff's Office.
Rio School District
The Rio School District, which covers kindergarten through 8th grade, serves both Nyeland Acres and El Rio.

01Is Nyeland Acres part of Oxnard?

No. It is an unincorporated community of Ventura County, outside the city limits, as the community section above lays out.

Any of the services the office lists. The services section above shows them, and the services page links to each one. Write down the name of the service before you call, so the dental office knows which one you mean.

03What should I do in a dental emergency?

Phone the dental office at (805) 366-6000. If you believe you are experiencing a medical emergency, call 911 or go to the nearest emergency room. See Emergency Dentistry for more.

The dental office, at (805) 366-6000. Insurance plans, payment options, fees and new patient forms are all questions for the office itself.
