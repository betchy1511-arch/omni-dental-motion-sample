What cosmetic dentistry covers

Understanding Cosmetic Dentistry

Cosmetic dentistry improves how your smile looks. At Omni Dental Specialty that includes professional teeth whitening, custom porcelain veneers, Invisalign clear aligners and dental bonding.

Each one fixes a different kind of flaw. Whitening lightens the color of your teeth. Veneers and bonding cover chips, gaps and discoloration. Invisalign gradually moves teeth into proper alignment.

Dr. Keihani uses advanced digital imaging to create a visual preview of how your smile can improve with different cosmetic treatments, so you can make informed decisions about your dental care.

Why patients choose cosmetic care

What Cosmetic Dentistry Can Do for Your Smile

A smile you are happy with can give you more confidence. Each cosmetic treatment targets a different concern, so you can choose what fits your goals.

  • A brighter shade. Professional whitening in the office or at home.
  • Chips and gaps covered. Veneers correct discoloration, chips, gaps and mild misalignment.
  • Straighter teeth, discreetly. Invisalign is an alternative to metal braces.
  • Quick fixes for minor flaws. Bonding costs less and carries fewer risks.

01How do I choose the right cosmetic treatment?

Dr. Keihani uses advanced digital imaging to create a visual preview of how your smile can improve with different cosmetic treatments, so you can make an informed decision.

02Is professional whitening different from store-bought products?

Over-the-counter products often give limited results. Professional-grade whitening can lighten your teeth by several shades in a single visit, and you can choose in-office or take-home whitening.

03What can porcelain veneers fix?

Veneers are ultra-thin shells that cover the front surface of your teeth. They can correct discoloration, chips, gaps and mild misalignment.

04What is dental bonding?

Bonding uses tooth-colored composite resin to improve chipped, cracked, discolored or gapped teeth. It is a quick option with lower costs and fewer risks.

05Is Invisalign an alternative to braces?

Yes. Invisalign is a discreet alternative to traditional metal braces. It uses a series of custom-made, nearly invisible aligners to gradually move your teeth into proper alignment.
