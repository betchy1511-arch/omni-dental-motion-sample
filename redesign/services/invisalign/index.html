How Invisalign works

Understanding Invisalign

Invisalign uses a series of clear aligners to gently move your teeth into alignment.

It is a modern alternative to traditional braces, without the visibility of metal brackets and wires. Invisalign can address crossbite, gaps between teeth, crowding, overbite, underbite and open bite, and it can correct mild to moderate misalignments.

Treatment time depends on your individual needs and ranges from a few months to a few years.

Step by step

How the Invisalign Process Works

Every step of your treatment is mapped out before your first set of aligners is made.

  1. 01

    Digital Scan

    Your dentist takes a detailed digital scan of your teeth to create a personalized treatment plan.

  2. 02

    Custom Aligners

    Each step of your treatment is mapped out, and custom aligners are made to guide your teeth into position.

  3. 03

    Wearing Your Aligners

    Wear each set about 22 hours a day, removing it only to eat, brush or floss. Every two weeks, you switch to the next set.

  4. 04

    Your Aligned Smile

    In as little as nine months, you can enjoy a straighter, more balanced smile.

Treatment time varies with your individual needs, ranging from a few months to a few years.

Why patients choose Invisalign

Why Invisalign Is a Comfortable Choice

Invisalign offers several advantages over traditional braces, for a healthier, more confident smile.

  • Comfortable. Smooth, medical-grade plastic with no brackets or wires to irritate.
  • Nearly invisible. Clear aligners keep your treatment discreet.
  • Faster in some cases. Up to 50% faster than traditional braces in some cases.
  • Easy oral care. Take the aligners out to brush and floss.
