How an implant works

Understanding Dental Implants

Dental implants are a long-term solution for replacing missing teeth. They consist of a small titanium post that is surgically placed into the jawbone, where it fuses naturally with the bone over time. This creates a strong, stable foundation for a replacement tooth, such as a crown, bridge, or denture.

Unlike removable dentures or bridges, implants are fixed in place and function just like natural teeth.

At Omni Dental Specialty, we use only high-quality materials backed by scientific research to ensure your implant looks natural and lasts for years to come.

Step by step

How the Dental Implant Process Works

Getting a dental implant involves several important steps, all carefully managed by our experienced team.

  1. 01

    Exam and 3D Imaging

    It begins with a thorough examination and advanced 3D imaging to assess your jawbone and oral health.

  2. 02

    Preparing the Site

    If needed, we can perform preparatory treatments such as bone grafting to ensure a strong foundation.

  3. 03

    Placing the Implant

    Next, the implant is placed into your jawbone during a minor surgical procedure. Over the next few months, your body naturally integrates the implant through a process called osseointegration.

  4. 04

    Your New Tooth

    Once healing is complete, we attach a connector piece called an abutment and then secure your custom-made replacement tooth.

Throughout the process, we follow strict safety protocols and provide detailed aftercare to ensure a smooth recovery and long-term success.

Why patients choose implants

Why Dental Implants Are Worth It

Dental implants can transform how you eat, speak, and feel. Because they’re anchored into your jaw, implants allow you to bite and chew with confidence, making it easier to enjoy a full, healthy diet.

  • Fixed in place. They don’t shift, click, or require adhesives.
  • Stands on its own. You don’t have to worry about slipping dentures or damaging nearby teeth, as implants stand independently and require no support from adjacent teeth.
  • Replaces the root. Because they replace both the visible tooth and the root underneath, implants help preserve bone and maintain the natural shape of your face and jawline.
  • A long-term investment. Many of our patients tell us they feel more confident, more comfortable, and more like themselves after their implant treatment.

Safety and recovery

Safety and Recovery With Dental Implants

Yes, dental implants are considered one of the safest and most predictable dental procedures available today. When placed by trained professionals, like the team at our Oxnard clinic, implants have a very high success rate and a low risk of complications.

As with any medical procedure, there are some risks, such as infection or implant failure. However, these are rare and usually preventable with proper care and planning. At Omni Dental Specialty, we use advanced technology like 3D imaging to minimize risks and carefully evaluate each patient’s needs before beginning treatment.

If you have medical conditions like diabetes or are a smoker, we’ll discuss how these factors may affect healing and success. Our team provides thorough follow-up care and clear instructions to support your recovery every step of the way.
